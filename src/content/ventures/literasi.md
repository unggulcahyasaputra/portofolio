Literasi.top is a digital literacy initiative dedicated to bridging the digital divide in Indonesia.

## Focus Areas
- Digital Safety Education
- Internet Wellness
- Technology Tutorials
- Educator Resources
- Community Outreach

## Mission
To ensure every Indonesian has the digital literacy skills needed to thrive in the modern world.
